How it works

From surplus to served in four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Provider lists food

    Add the item, quantity, expiry time and a photo. Choose free donation or discounted sale.

  2. 2

    NGO discovers & claims

    Verified NGOs browse nearby listings sorted by distance and claim what they need.

  3. 3

    Pickup or delivery

    The NGO collects it themselves, or requests a volunteer to bring it over.

  4. 4

    Receipt generated

    Both sides get an automatic receipt, so every rescue is recorded.

Frequently asked questions

Who can list food?

Restaurants, bakeries, grocers, caterers and any shop with safe, surplus food. Providers upload a food licence (such as FSSAI) when registering.

Is it free to use?

Registering and browsing are free. Providers decide whether each listing is a free donation or a discounted sale.

How does an NGO get the food?

Claim a listing, then choose self pickup or request a volunteer delivery. A receipt is created automatically.

What happens if food isn't claimed in time?

Listings are marked as expired once their expiry time passes, so NGOs only see food that's still good to collect.

Can I volunteer without a vehicle?

Volunteers pick delivery requests that suit them, including short local trips.
